<objective>
Promote live shows to listeners and let them opt in or out of watching.

Purpose: show-only video is only valuable if listeners are told a show is live —
and only if they can silence that. This plan owns the prompt/promotion and the
global on/off preference.
Output: a per-user `watch_video` preference, an in-app promote banner, and push
on show start.
</objective>
