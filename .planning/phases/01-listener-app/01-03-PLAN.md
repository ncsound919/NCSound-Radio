<objective>
Attribute listener requests to the signed-in user and provide in-app account
deletion (an App Store requirement).

Purpose: `POST /api/requests` currently trusts a free-text `listenerName`; the
app needs a real identity, and guideline 5.1.1(v) requires account deletion.
Output: a JWT-aware requests route and a `delete-account` Edge Function.
</objective>
