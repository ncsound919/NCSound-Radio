<objective>
Deploy the public stream relay and retarget the home encoder at it.

Purpose: nothing in the app is buildable until a phone on cellular can play the
stream over HTTPS. The relay is a dumb Icecast (`infra/stream/`) that re-serves
the home Liquidsoap's two mounts over TLS.
Output: a live `stream.<domain>` and a `ncsound.liq` that pushes to it.
</objective>

- **T2 (medium)** Stream served over HTTP → ATS failure and cleartext. Mitigation: TLS only; no plain listener port.
</threat_model>
